April 30 · Block M · Mabopane

Re tshwanela go nna moo.
We are the rightful people of this place.

That's what one elder said before the workshop began. Not as a statement — as a fact. Sixty people gathered, field guides came out, and the stream course was mapped and surveyed. By the end of the day, 9 invasive species had been identified, labelled, and removed.
