Solar Floodlights Installed at Block M Waterfall Park

25 April 2026 · Bra Tshego — MGSN

Two 12-metre pressure-treated timber poles were installed this week in 600mm drilled holes. Each pole carries a solar panel and IK10-rated vandal-resistant LED floodlight.

Five-zone anti-climb system: thorny indigenous planting at base, non-drying anti-climb paint from 0.5m to 3m, welded steel skirt collars at 3m, star-crown barbed wire halos at 10m, and welded mesh panel cages protecting solar equipment.

Why this matters: After-dark lighting transforms the park from a perceived vacant lot into actively managed community infrastructure. It deters illegal dumping and signals that the space is cared for.

Next step: community notice board at the shelter and compost bay construction scheduled for May session.

Volunteer Registration Now Open

24 April 2026 · MGSN Management

The MGSN Clean-Up Team-Up volunteer programme is officially open. Volunteers commit to fortnightly Saturday sessions (08:00–11:00) at the Block M Waterfall Timber Shelter.

Available roles: Session Lead, Grass Cutter, Beds Keeper, Compost Sorter, Shelter Steward, Structural/Construction, and General Volunteer.

Rewards: Every session earns points redeemable for vouchers and gift cards from local businesses including Shoprite, Cashbuild, and food vendors along the stream. 5 sessions = R50 grocery voucher. 8 sessions = R100 partner voucher.

Register using the volunteer form or scan the QR code at the timber shelter. All community members are welcome.

UP Architecture Partnership Confirmed

15 April 2026 · MGSN Board

The University of Pretoria Department of Architecture has formally confirmed its partnership with MGSN for the design of the Block M green corridor and integrated stormwater management project.

The UP team provides design expertise, student research capacity, and institutional credibility to MGSN's municipal engagement process with the City of Tshwane. Initial site visits and design workshops are planned for Semester 2, 2026.

What this means: Positions MGSN's work as technically credible urban infrastructure — not merely civic activism. Opens pathways to municipal budget allocation, climate adaptation funding, and formal land-use integration.

Community members with architecture, engineering, or urban planning skills who wish to contribute are encouraged to contact MGSN directly.
